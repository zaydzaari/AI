# The rules behind this refinement

Reviewed 3 November 2026. These are paraphrased principles from primary sources, followed by our application to Aether. They are established foundations, not a claim that classic advice was published in the last two months.

| Source | Useful principle | Applied here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dieter Rams / Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design) | Make the product understandable; remove things that distract from its purpose; resolve small details carefully. | The conversation carries 01A. Decorative header badges and repeated reassurance text are removed. The annotation attaches to the exact model switch it explains. |
| [Steve Schoger and Adam Wathan / Refactoring UI](https://refactoringui.com/) | Establish relative importance, group with space, reduce unnecessary borders, and use consistent spacing and type choices. | Quieter navigation, a stronger headline, compact model indexes, open price columns and fewer divider lines. Each concept gets an appropriate default heading weight. |
| [Matthew Butterick / Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/typography-in-ten-minutes.html) | Text quality depends on the relationship between size, typeface, line length and leading. Judge the actual text rather than applying a font size blindly. | Readable demo copy, shorter measures, tuned headline wrapping, face-specific weights and a shorter mobile transcript. |
| [Kelley Gordon / Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/) | Scale, contrast, balance and proximity guide attention and clarify groups. | The hero has a clear entry point; model choices and pricing use different compositions. Label, response and annotation have distinct roles. |

The warm paper, restrained rust accent, serif notes and annotated product remain the direction for Field Notes. Other concepts retain their established palettes and typefaces. No universal ban on a colour, font, border or shadow substitutes for a visual judgment.
